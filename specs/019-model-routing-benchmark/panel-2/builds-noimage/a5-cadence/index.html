What it does

Three mechanisms, one beat.

Not a dashboard on top of your CI — the thing that actually paces the release.

Release trains

Bundle changes into a fixed weekly or nightly window instead of an unplanned queue. Merge whenever — Cadence lines everything up for the next scheduled departure.

Flag-timed rollout

Ramp a feature flag on a schedule — 10% now, 50% tomorrow, 100% Thursday — without hand-editing a config at 6am.

Automatic rollback net

Cadence watches the error budget through every stage of a rollout and reverts the flag the moment it's breached — before a human has to notice.

How it works

Four steps, on repeat.

The same beat every train, every week.

  1. Configure

    Describe your release train once, in YAML — flags, windows, rollback thresholds.

  2. Schedule

    Pick a cadence: nightly, weekly, or a window that matches your team's calendar.

  3. Ship

    Cadence opens the window and ramps the flag on schedule — no manual trigger.

  4. Verify

    Error budgets are watched automatically; a breach rolls back without paging anyone.

There's a train on Thursday at 2 pm. You're either on it, or you wait for the next one — nobody argues about when to deploy.
