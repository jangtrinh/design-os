How it works

From one commit to one pulse

No new pipeline to author. Cadence attaches to the stages your CI already runs and turns their output into a single trace per branch.

  1. Step 1

    Commit

    A push lands and your CI kicks off the run it always does. Cadence listens to that run — build, test, deploy stages — without a new config file or a second pipeline to maintain.

  2. Step 2

    Pulse check

    Every stage reports a beat: duration, pass or fail, and how it compares to that same stage's last ten runs. Cadence collapses that into one trace instead of ten dashboards to cross-reference.

  3. Step 3

    Ship

    A steady pulse ships itself if you've wired auto-promote. An irregular one pages the person who owns that stage — with the one trace that explains why, not a bare "something broke."

What Cadence believes

Three decisions the product won't walk back

  • 01

    One trace beats ten dashboards

    Cadence doesn't add another screen to check. It collapses pipeline signal into one pulse per branch, so "is it fine" has one answer, not ten tabs.

  • 02

    Silence means healthy

    You hear from Cadence when the beat changes, not on a fixed schedule. No daily digest, no green checkmark you have to go find and confirm.

  • 03

    Every alert carries its evidence

    A page from Cadence includes the run it's about and the runs it's compared against — never a bare "something's wrong, go look."
