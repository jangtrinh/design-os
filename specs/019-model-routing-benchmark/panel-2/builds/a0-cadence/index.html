Configuration

Declare it once. Cadence enforces it every time.

A single cadence.yml describes your train schedule, rollout curve, and the health signals that decide whether to promote or pull back.

  • Version-controlled — your release policy lives in the repo, reviewed like code.
  • Environment-aware — different gates for staging, canary, and production.
  • Signal-driven — wire in latency, error rate, or a custom metric as a gate.
cadence.yml 
train: nightly
schedule: "0 2 * * 1-5"  # weekdays, 2am
rollout:
  - stage: canary
    weight: 5
    bake: 10m
  - stage: partial
    weight: 25
    bake: 20m
  - stage: full
    weight: 100
gates:
  - metric: p99_latency_ms
    max: 300
  - metric: error_rate
    max: 0.5
on_gate_fail: rollback
