How a grant moves

One secret, one scope, one duration — followed end to end.

  1. Request

    A service or person asks for exactly what it needs — one secret, one scope, one duration.

  2. Grant

    Haven opens a scoped, time-boxed lease. No standing access, no shared password to remember.

  3. Rotate

    When the lease closes, Haven rotates the credential on its own. Nothing left over to forget about.

Every read is on the record

A specimen of Haven's access log — who touched what, and what happened to it.

What Haven refuses to do

No invented guarantees — just the design decisions that make standing access impossible.

  • Scoped, not standing

    A grant names exactly one secret and one action. Nothing is handed out "just in case."

  • Expires by default

    Every grant carries a duration. If nobody renews it, Haven closes it — nobody has to remember to.

  • Every read is attributable

    Each access ties back to a person or a service identity. A shared password is never in the picture.
