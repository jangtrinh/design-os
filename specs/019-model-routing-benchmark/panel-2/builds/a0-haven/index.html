End-to-end encryption

Fine-grained access

How it works

From a raw credential to a governed one, in three steps.

01

Store it once

Push a secret from your terminal, CI job, or the dashboard. It's encrypted before it leaves your machine.

# from anywhere
haven push STRIPE_KEY --env prod
02

Grant precisely

Attach a policy: which service, which environment, which window of time. Nothing is implicitly shared.

haven grant ci-deploy \
--secret STRIPE_KEY --ttl 24h
03

Rotate without fear

Set a rotation cadence once. Haven handles issuance, propagation, and retirement of the old value.

haven rotate STRIPE_KEY --every 90d ✓ scheduled
Security architecture

Designed so that even we can't read your secrets.

Haven uses a zero-knowledge architecture: encryption and decryption happen on your device, keyed to material we never see. What lands on our servers is ciphertext, full stop.

  • Client-side AES-256-GCM

    Every secret is sealed before it leaves your device, using keys derived from your organization's own key hierarchy.

  • Hardware-backed key storage

    Root keys are held in HSMs with strict split-knowledge controls — no single engineer can reconstruct them.

  • Independent annual audits

    Penetration tests and SOC 2 Type II attestations are performed by third parties and available under NDA.

  • Break-glass, logged

    Emergency access exists for account recovery only, requires dual approval, and generates an unsuppressable audit entry.
