Secrets, on a ticket

You don't get a password. You get a ticket.

Haven issues a scoped, time-boxed claim ticket for one secret at a time, then tears it up when the clock runs out. No shared .env file, no standing key, no crossed fingers.

Principles

  • Nothing standing, only issued.

    Haven has no permanent password to steal. Every credential exists as a live grant with an owner and a clock, never as a row in a shared vault everyone can read.

  • Every ticket expires itself.

    Expiry isn't a policy someone has to remember to enforce. It's a property of the ticket — when the clock runs out, the value is gone, whether or not anyone was watching.

  • The stub outlives the ticket.

    The ticket disappears; the record of it doesn't. Every issue, use, and expiry writes one line to a ledger nobody can edit after the fact.
