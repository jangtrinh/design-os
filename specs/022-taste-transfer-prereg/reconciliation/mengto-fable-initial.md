## 1. Independent taxonomy — the 4-kind split is under-resolved

The prior report's functional split (A style cards / B technique recipes / C reference→artifact pipelines / D meta-governance) is directionally right for C and D but **wrong at its most consequential point**: bucket A ("~60 style cards, lowest reuse value, exactly what personas + signature-devices own"). Reading the cards shows bucket A is actually **four different kinds of artifact**, and only one of them shadows the persona library:

- **A1 — Micro-device cards (~25–30 files).** `number-details`, `container-lines`, `corner-diagonals`, `corner-lasers`, `marquee-loop`, `masked-reveal`, `progressive-blur`, `beam-glow-states`, `css-border-gradient`, `gooey-blob-system`, `liquid-metal-border`, `reveal-hover-effect`, `scroll-progress-timeline`, `staggered-word-reveal`, `dither-background`, `atmosphere-background`, … [F] These are **signature-device-grade**, not persona-grade. They are the direct analog of `signature-devices.md` entries (which itself already contains "marquee ticker" and "hand-drawn annotation" at exactly this granularity). DESIGN:OS carries **9 devices**; MengTo carries roughly **30 device candidates**. Rejecting these as "persona shadowing" (R1) is a category error — a device menu does not compete with a persona library, it **feeds** it. [I]
- **A2 — Aesthetic-system cards (~20–25 files).** `editorial-tech`, `light-mode-paper-technical`, `nested-container-clean-agency`, `technical-wireframe-info-layout`, `skeuomorphic-ui`, `glass-dark-ui`, `orange-clean-paper-saas`, … These ARE persona-adjacent. ## 6. Taste-transfer pilot design [R]

**Question:** does MengTo-derived taste knowledge, adapted to house form, improve DESIGN:OS generation quality on matched briefs under blind owner judgment?

- **Unit of transfer:** a *knowledge patch* — an adapted candidate in house format, one tranche per arm-pair:
  - **T1 (aesthetics):** 3 aesthetic/genre cards adapted into candidate persona-DNA form — `editorial-tech`, `light-mode-paper-technical`, `documentary-brutalist-agency` (chosen for spread: light/dark × editorial/technical/brutalist, and minimal overlap with existing 26).
  - **T2 (motion):** genre-scoped bands + cinematic T5 base tokens patched into persona motion DNA + `gsap-motion-direction.md`, provenance-marked, labelled a lower evidence tier than `SOURCE`.
  - **T3 (devices):** 4–6 micro-devices appended to `signature-devices.md` in house device format (e.g. number-details, container-lines/corner-brackets, progressive-blur, scroll-progress-timeline).
- **Candidates selected by:** coverage of an existing DESIGN:OS gap (checked against persona-index/device list), not popularity; Neuform HTML excluded entirely (R7).
- **Design:** paired A/B per brief. Control = current knowledge core; treatment = control + one tranche. **Exactly one variable per arm-pair** (tranche level). 6 briefs × 2 arms = 12 renders, briefs written to plausibly activate the patch (e.g. an editorial-tech-adjacent product page for T1; a cinematic scroll story for T2). Same model, runtime, asset policy, time budget. All 12 outputs must pass the **full linter set** before judging, so the floors are equal in both arms and the judged delta is taste, not compliance.
- **Contradiction probe (from spec 018):** one brief where the patch is out of scope (a data-dense dashboard where cinematic timing would be wrong); treatment must NOT leak the patch. A leak fails the tranche regardless of wins.
- **Blinding:** renders anonymized, A/B randomized per pair (coin flips preregistered). **Owner judges pairwise** (winner + 1–5 confidence). Secondary: a blind curator pass scores the 7 rubric axes. Machine settles floors only.
- **Artifacts:** preregistration file (briefs, randomization, thresholds — committed before any render), 12 renders + screenshots, judgment sheet, verdict per tranche.
- **Falsification:** a tranche fails if treatment wins < 4/6 pairs, or any contradiction-probe leak, or any floor regression. A failing tranche is recorded and **not** retried with tweaked values (that is overfitting to the judge).
- **Adoption path on win:** the tranche graduates through the librarian veto chain (gap → judge → human merge) with `<!-- ease:source ref="references/mengto-skills/…" captured="202607" url="github.com/MengTo/Skills" -->` provenance; the studied SKILL.md texts (MIT, Meng To-authored) are vendored under `references/`, never `knowledge/` directly.
- **Spec 018 reuse: yes [F].** `benchmark-contract.md`'s controlled protocol — single-variable arms, identical everything else, anonymous randomized evaluation, contradiction + isolation cases, preserve all attempts, "0→0 never counts as improvement" — is directly reusable; swap the treatment variable from "recalled learning" to "MengTo patch." Thresholds should be re-set for this pilot (win-rate, not the 018 graduation deltas, which measure a different loop).
- **Cost:** ~½ day adaptation per tranche + 12 renders on the existing pipeline + ~45 min owner judging ≈ **1–2 days total**, versus Brief #1's multi-week program.
