# Documentary-Brutalist Genre Playbook

**Contract**
- Open with a billboard-scale positioning line and minimal navigation.
- Alternate hard black chapters (large titles, role labels, documentary media) with sparse warm-white thinking chapters.
- One compressed display face paired with one neutral grotesk.
- Exposed grid, square corners; no glass, glow, or decorative gradients.
- Line breaks are composition — verify at every breakpoint.
- One documentary collage as a paced process story: reserve every image box before load, keep DOM order narrative even when the visual grid is irregular, cap parallax below 5%, captions on hover AND focus, grid survives failed media.
- Pale proof surfaces only for verified quotes.
- Disclosure controls semantic and accessible (`aria-expanded`, no focus traps outside true modals).
- Oversized footer wordmark.

**Context**
Agencies, studios, cultural institutions, portfolios, investigations — surfaces earning a strong point of view.

**Anti-context**
Forms, checkout, help, appendices, and any care/consumer-warm or urgent surface — calm, legible, conventional treatment there.

---

This guidance is a contextual band, never a global default. Passing a deterministic check adds no taste credit.
