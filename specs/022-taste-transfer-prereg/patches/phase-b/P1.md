# Role-to-Ratio and Text-Reserve Media Contract

**Contract**
- Declare a role for every image before selection, and let role fix ratio — avatar 1:1 (256/512/1024 square exports); headshot 4:5 or 3:4, shoulders-up, faces centered; wide hero/background 16:9 with calm negative space; story/mobile 9:16.
- On wide imagery carrying text, reserve 30–50% of the frame as calm space for the message.
- Keep faces' eyes in the upper third, never crop chin or forehead, preserve horizon lines.
- Verify focal and text safety at 390/768/1440, not by file ratio alone.
- Provide resolution variants per role.

**Context**
Surfaces with supplied or role-approved imagery — campaigns, editorial, product launches.

**Anti-context**
Data, legal, console, and form surfaces get no decorative imagery; the contract never justifies inventing an image — intentional no-image is a first-class outcome.

---

This guidance is a contextual band, never a global default. Passing a deterministic check adds no taste credit.
