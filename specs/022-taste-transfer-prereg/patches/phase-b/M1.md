# Brutalist Contextual Motion Band

**Contract**
- Controls answer in 160–220 ms.
- Masks and section entrances take 500–760 ms.
- Animate one reading transition at a time.
- Slow collage parallax capped below 5%.
- Prefer decisive cuts over floating.
- Pause offscreen observers and media, clean up route timelines.
- Never smooth-scroll theater or scroll hijack.

**Context**
Documentary/brutalist narrative surfaces.

**Anti-context**
Forms and dense operational surfaces — instant feedback only, zero entrance choreography.

---

Additive to the knowledge core. Accessibility, tap reliability, and performance outrank this guidance. Reduced motion renders settled states immediately.

This guidance is a contextual band, never a global default. Passing a deterministic check adds no taste credit.
