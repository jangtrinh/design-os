# Cinematic Scrub Calibration

**Contract**
- Scroll-scrub factor 0.8–1.4 for cinematic delay.
- Reveals trigger when an element's top crosses roughly 82% of the viewport.
- Smooth-scroll interpolation around 0.08.
- Word stagger 35–70 ms, line stagger 80–140 ms, card stagger 60–100 ms.
- Staggered motion guides reading order, never decorates it.

**Context**
Long-form cinematic scroll narratives.

**Anti-context**
Utility blocks, search/index surfaces, tables, forms — native scrolling, instant anchors, no scrub; reduced motion renders the settled story.

---

Additive to the knowledge core. Accessibility, tap reliability, and performance outrank this guidance. Reduced motion renders settled states immediately.

This guidance is a contextual band, never a global default. Passing a deterministic check adds no taste credit.
