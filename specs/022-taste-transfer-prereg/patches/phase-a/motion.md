# Motion — Family Patch (Phase A)

This guidance is additive to the committed knowledge core. Apply it only inside its context boundary. Select at most one register/band/device treatment per surface as its own boundary directs; never blend registers on one surface. Outside the boundary, or where the anti-context matches, this guidance is inactive and the knowledge core alone governs. Accessibility, tap reliability, and performance always outrank this guidance.

## Band one

**Contract**
- Controls answer in 160–220 ms.
- Masks and section entrances take 500–760 ms.
- Animate one reading transition at a time.
- Slow collage parallax capped below 5%.
- Prefer decisive cuts over floating.
- Pause offscreen observers and media, clean up route timelines.
- Never smooth-scroll theater or scroll hijack.

**Context**
Documentary/brutalist narrative surfaces.

**Anti-context**
Forms and dense operational surfaces — instant feedback only, zero entrance choreography.

## Band two

**Contract**
- Control feedback 160–220 ms.
- Interface state changes 420–700 ms.
- Card stagger 45–70 ms.
- Animate one handoff at a time.
- Background drift slow, optional, offscreen-paused.
- No fake typing, perpetual floating, or decorative loops.
- Render settled UI immediately under reduced motion.

**Context**
Product-proof, workflow, and demo surfaces.

**Anti-context**
Reference/data sections and forms — static.

## Band three

**Contract**
- Scroll-scrub factor 0.8–1.4 for cinematic delay.
- Reveals trigger when an element's top crosses roughly 82% of the viewport.
- Smooth-scroll interpolation around 0.08.
- Word stagger 35–70 ms, line stagger 80–140 ms, card stagger 60–100 ms.
- Staggered motion guides reading order, never decorates it.

**Context**
Long-form cinematic scroll narratives.

**Anti-context**
Utility blocks, search/index surfaces, tables, forms — native scrolling, instant anchors, no scrub; reduced motion renders the settled story.

## Selection rule

Choose the band matching the surface genre — documentary/brutalist narrative → band one; product-proof/workflow → band two; cinematic long-form story → band three. Forms and dense data surfaces get instant feedback (≤220 ms) and no entrance choreography regardless of band. Reduced motion renders settled states immediately.

---

These are contextual bands, never global defaults. Passing a deterministic check adds no taste credit.
