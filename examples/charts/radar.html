Streaming engine capability profiles

Three candidate engines scored 0–5 on five shared criteria. Helix is the recommendation: it is the only profile that holds 4 or better on throughput, latency and reliability at once, and it pays for that in cost efficiency.

Illustrative dataset — the scores are invented to demonstrate the radar grammar and describe no real product evaluation. All five criteria share one 0–5 scale with the centre as a true zero; no axis is normalised separately and no inner ring is lifted off zero. Vertex marker shape, not colour, is the primary series cue, and the key repeats each shape. Overlap is handled with fill-opacity on a token colour. Colour resolves through design-system tokens; where no project tokens are compiled the page falls back to the documented neutral palette declared in :root. Static, script-free, and offline.
