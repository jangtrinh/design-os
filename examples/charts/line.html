Monthly active users, 2025

Total monthly active users grew from 128k to 232k across the year, with mobile taking a steadily larger share. Both series share one axis, one unit, and a zero baseline.

Illustrative dataset — the figures are invented to demonstrate the line grammar and describe no real product. Units are stated once, on the value axis. The baseline is zero and the axis is not truncated; the scale is 2 px per thousand users. Samples are joined as a polyline, never smoothed, and no gap is bridged. Series are told apart by dash pattern and a direct end label as well as by colour. Colour resolves through design-system tokens; where no project tokens are compiled the page falls back to the documented neutral palette declared in :root. Static, script-free, and offline.
