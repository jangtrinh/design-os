## What was adopted, and what changed on the way in

The owner ruled on every conflict with this repository's doctrine (2026-10-29):

- The conversion layer (intake, the fixed section skeleton, conversion copy, the tagline
  section, SEO and the ship list) applies to **every landing page**.
- The fixed visual values became a **named persona**, chosen when no project design system
  exists. A project design system and its soul still win, as in `generation-craft-defaults.md`.
- Realistic people, brands and numbers are allowed **only as labelled sample data**; shipped
  claims stay sourced (constitution Article VIII).
- Values that broke an accessibility floor were kept and **corrected to pass it** (constitution
  Article X): button height, the muted tagline start colour, remote placeholder imagery, and the
  fix order of an upgrade audit.
