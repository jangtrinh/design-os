# Persona families (`families.json`)

A family's declared accent (`starting_tokens.tokens.color.accent.$value`) is legitimate for the generated-UI tells when `ui gate --family <slug>` names that family: `ai-color-palette` reports a hue within ±15° of it as "family accent (`<slug>`)", not a generic AI tell.

Families under `ui persona lint`'s screens-per-app floor are provisional until thickened with more real corpus coverage; a `history` entry whose note says "thin" or "provisional" records that gap as a decision, not an accident.
